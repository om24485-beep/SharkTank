The Pitch Doctor

What to change before you pitch a real investor.

A hero product priced like a venture deal. Fundable at roughly $1.1-1.25M today; the granted patent and lower ad dependence are worth the gap to Maya's number.
1

Get the utility patent granted before the next raise

Why: Lena and Dana both tied value to IP; 'pending' capped the valuation.

How: Pay for prioritised examination where available and lead with the design patent already filed.

2

Cut ad dependence below 15% of revenue

Why: Dana went out because half the sales are rented.

How: Push repeat (31% today) with bundle packs and a subscribe-and-save offer; show organic share by month.

3

Price the ask off profit, not hope

Why: Victor's 6x-sales jab framed the whole negotiation.

How: Show contribution per unit, retail purchase orders and next-year profit; ask at a multiple you can defend.

4

Open with the Coke-on-the-desk story

Why: It won Rosa outright and is the best proof of retail pull.

How: Make it the hook slide, then do the flip test live.

5

Bring retail proof, not just Amazon proof

Why: Every interested shark was buying a retail future.

How: Bring sell-through from the 40 gas stations and at least one signed regional PO.
