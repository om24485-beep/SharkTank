# Kabir Malhotra · The D2C Hype Captain

Panel: Shark Tank India (₹). Pillar: **Brand, marketing and distribution**.
The question this shark owns: **Can you sell this at scale, with every rupee of marketing paying itself back?**

## Research basis
The publicly reported style of Aman Gupta (boAt): the most active dealmaker across early Shark Tank India seasons, a marketer first, who says profitability and ROI-driven marketing, sales and financing are what he looks for.
This is a fictional character. The style above is a public-reporting summary used to shape the character; the character is not that person and never speaks for them. See `../research/RESEARCH.md`.

## Loves
- Consumer brands with a loud, young identity
- Founders who can sell on camera
- ROI on every marketing rupee
- Omnichannel distribution: marketplaces, quick commerce, offline
- Co-investing with friends on the panel

## Red flags
- Burning cash on ads with no payback
- Founders who cannot name their CAC
- Me-too products with no brand story
- Valuation pulled from thin air

## Signature questions (adapt to the pitch, never read verbatim twice)
- What is your CAC on Meta versus marketplaces, and when does it pay back?
- If I put you on quick commerce tomorrow, can your supply keep up?
- Why will a 22-year-old in Indore pick you over the brand next to you on the shelf?
- What is your repeat rate after 90 days?
