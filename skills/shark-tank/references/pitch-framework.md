# Pitch framework

A Shark Tank pitch is spoken, short and built to survive interruption. Six to eight slides, two to three minutes out loud.

| # | Kicker | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hook | Make the sharks lean in within 15 seconds | Open with a number, a moment or a demo, never "Hi, I'm…". |
| 2 | The problem | Make the pain specific | One customer, one situation, one cost. |
| 3 | The solution | Show it working | Say what it does in one sentence; bullets for how. A live demo line if possible. |
| 4 | Traction | Prove someone wants it | Real numbers only: revenue, users, retention, pilots, waitlist. Put 2-4 in `stats`. If there is no traction, say so and show the strongest signal (LOIs, waitlist, pilot interest). |
| 5 | Business model | Show how money comes in | Price, unit cost, margin, payback. |
| 6 | Why us | Founder-market fit | Lived experience, unfair insight, the team. |
| 7 | Competition (optional) | Name the obvious rival before a shark does | One line on why you win. |
| 8 | Market (optional) | Only if size is not obvious | Bottom-up beats a top-down TAM. |

Then **the ask**: amount, equity, implied valuation, use of funds (three buckets max, percentages summing to 100), and one line on what kind of shark they want.

## Writing the `say` lines
- Spoken English (or the founder's natural Hinglish for an Indian panel if the brief suggests it), 25 to 60 words per slide.
- Concrete nouns and numbers. No "revolutionary", "disrupting", "game-changing", "leveraging".
- Every number in `say` must match `stats` and the brief.

## Picking the ask when the user gave none
- Pre-revenue idea: raise 12 to 18 months of a lean burn; equity 5-15% (USA) or 2-10% (India).
- Revenue: price at a defensible multiple from `scoring.md` and round to a TV-friendly number (₹50 lakh, ₹1 crore; $100k, $250k, $500k).
- Mark the ask `(proposed by founder agent)` in `episode.json.assumptions`.

## The 60-second elevator pitch
One paragraph, 90 to 130 words: problem, solution, proof, model, ask. This is what the Pitch Doctor rewrites at the end.
